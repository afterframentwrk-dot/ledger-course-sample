What we do

Services

We build and repair outdoor masonry in the Triad. Every job starts with a site visit, a written scope, and a schedule you can plan around.

Outdoor living

Patios & outdoor rooms

A patio should feel like another room, not a slab that ponds after a storm. We set flagstone, pavers, or brick on a compacted base, pitch the surface away from the house, and finish the edges so the lawn and beds meet the stone cleanly.

  • Flagstone, paver, and brick patios
  • Built-in seating walls and steps
  • Outdoor kitchens and grill islands
  • Bases, drainage, and edge restraints

Brick & stone

Masonry & stone veneer

New work and careful matches to old work. We lay brick and stone, wrap fireplace surrounds, and repair chimneys, steps, and walls so the patch does not announce itself from the street.

  • Brick, block, and natural stone
  • Stone veneer on walls, columns, and fireplaces
  • Steps, stoops, and entry piers
  • Repointing and tuckpointing

Grade & circulation

Retaining walls, walks & steps

If the yard drops, the wall has to hold. We build structural and garden walls with drainage stone behind them, and we cut walkways and stairs that make the slope useful instead of awkward.

  • Segmental block and natural stone walls
  • Walkways in pavers, brick, or flagstone
  • Exterior stairs and landings
  • Drainage planning with every wall

Evenings outside

Fire pits & fireplaces

Wood or gas, built-in or a stone pad for a bowl. We keep clearances honest, build a solid base, and finish the surround so you can sit close without the thing looking like an afterthought.

  • Masonry fire pits and seating walls
  • Outdoor fireplace surrounds
  • Flagstone or paver pads for fire bowls
  • Repairs to existing fire features

Keep it standing

Repairs & repointing

Loose caps, failed mortar, a walk that has started to pitch toward the house. We will tell you if it needs a rebuild or a careful repair, and we will not sell you a new patio if the old one just needs to be reset.

  • Mortar repair and joint matching
  • Resetting sunken pavers and steps
  • Capstone and coping replacement
  • Small walls, pillars, and chimney work
